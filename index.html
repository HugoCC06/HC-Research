Investment approach

Start with the business.

My approach begins with understanding how a company makes money, why customers choose it, how the economics change at scale and whether the business can reinvest capital at attractive returns.

I then consider valuation and the assumptions required to justify the current market price. The objective is to distinguish a good company from a good investment.

Equity Research · Comparative Analysis

Datadog vs Dynatrace

Comparing two observability software businesses through growth, business model, operating performance, valuation and investment risk.

Investment preference

Dynatrace on a valuation-adjusted basis

Datadog has the faster growth profile, but the valuation framework used in this analysis places a much higher premium on that growth. Dynatrace provides exposure to the same broad observability theme at a substantially lower revenue multiple.

Key debate

Growth versus price

The central investment question is whether Datadog's stronger growth, platform expansion and usage-based economics are sufficient to compensate for the valuation premium attached to the business.

1. Executive summary

Datadog and Dynatrace operate in the observability software market, helping organisations monitor increasingly complex technology environments. Both businesses benefit from the growth of cloud infrastructure and the increasing importance of application, infrastructure and security monitoring.

The businesses differ meaningfully in how they monetise their platforms. Datadog's model is closely linked to customer usage and expansion, while Dynatrace has historically had a stronger enterprise orientation.

Datadog reported fiscal 2025 revenue of approximately $3.43 billion, representing 28% year-over-year growth. Dynatrace reported fiscal 2025 revenue of approximately $1.70 billion, representing 19% year-over-year growth. These figures are based on the companies' reported fiscal-year results. citeturn0search0turn0search3

The investment decision therefore depends on more than which company is growing faster. The question is how much of that future growth is already reflected in the share price.

Valuation is the key reason the analysis prefers Dynatrace. Datadog can be the better business while Dynatrace can still be the better investment if the market price embeds too much optimism about Datadog's future growth.

A rigorous valuation would test revenue growth, margins, reinvestment, terminal assumptions and discount rates rather than relying on a single multiple. The multiples above are therefore best viewed as the starting point for the debate.

8. Scenario framework

Bear case

Growth normalises faster

Enterprise technology budgets weaken, new workloads slow and high-growth software multiples compress.

Valuation becomes the main risk.
Base case

Healthy expansion

Cloud adoption continues, customers expand usage and both companies deliver durable but moderating growth.

Relative valuation drives preference.
Bull case

Platform expansion

Observability expands into adjacent products, customer spend rises and platform consolidation increases.

Higher growth supports premium multiples.

9. Key risks

01

Competition

The observability market is competitive and includes large technology platforms as well as specialist vendors.

02

Valuation

High-growth software businesses can experience significant multiple compression when growth expectations change.

03

Technology budgets

A reduction in enterprise technology spending could affect customer expansion and new workloads.

04

Execution

Product development, sales execution and platform adoption remain important to long-term growth.

05

Cloud costs

Infrastructure and third-party hosting costs can affect gross margins as usage scales.

06

Expectations

The investment outcome depends on the gap between actual performance and what the market already expects.

10. Conclusion

Datadog has the stronger reported growth profile and significant evidence of customer expansion. Its FY2025 results demonstrate the scale and momentum of the business.

Dynatrace is the slower-growing business in this comparison, but its reported financial performance remains strong and its lower valuation creates a different risk/reward profile.

The conclusion of this research is therefore not that Dynatrace is necessarily the better company. It is that the valuation-adjusted opportunity appears more attractive when the current framework is applied.

Portfolio rules

How position sizes should change

Position sizing should increase when the underlying business thesis strengthens, valuation becomes more attractive or the downside becomes better understood. It should decrease when the thesis deteriorates, valuation becomes excessive or the original assumptions no longer hold.

High-volatility positions should remain small enough that a permanent loss would not materially damage the overall portfolio. This is particularly relevant to ASTS and NIO.

Dividend yield should not be treated as the sole measure of attractiveness. The sustainability of the dividend, underlying cash flow, balance sheet and ability to grow distributions are more important than headline yield alone.

Investment philosophy

How I approach a company

01 · Understand

Understand the business

I start with the business model. What does the company sell, who pays for it, why customers buy it and what makes the economics attractive or unattractive?

02 · Analyse

Analyse the financials

Revenue growth, margins, cash conversion, capital intensity, balance-sheet strength and the quality of incremental returns help establish the underlying financial profile.

03 · Compare

Compare the alternatives

A company should be evaluated against competitors and against other opportunities available to an investor.

04 · Value

Estimate intrinsic value

I use valuation frameworks such as DCFs and trading multiples to connect business assumptions with an estimate of value.

05 · Challenge

Look for what could go wrong

The downside case matters. I try to identify the assumptions that would have to fail for the investment thesis to break.
